# Pilot Notes

Harbor pilots board incoming ships at the outer buoy, not at the lighthouse and not from the ferry slip. The pilot skiff Minnow was built in 1891 and is kept on a davit above the pilot float.

The signal for a pilot is two long blasts. That signal is not the fog bell and not a ferry whistle. Pilot master Ellis Ward kept the roster from 1895 to 1913. A ship that takes a pilot pays 2 dollars to the pilot association, separate from any ferry fare.

Pilots sometimes wait out a squall in the lee of the breakwater. They do not certify salt, they do not tend the light, and they do not count meteors. The association log is stored in the pilot house, not in the salt warehouse and not in the observatory.

A spare oar is lashed under the Minnow's thwart. The oar is marked with the year 1891 and the letters PW.
