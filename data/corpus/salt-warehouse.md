# Salt Warehouse

Warehouse 3 holds the municipal salt reserve, behind a brick wall that replaced the timber lost in the September 1899 fire. The fire started in the net loft next door and did not reach the salt floor.

Noah Briggs certified the 1904 municipal salt harvest at 1,840 bushels. Cooperage rules require white oak barrels, six iron hoops, and a stamp of the harvest year. Moisture above 4 percent is grounds to reject a lot. The salt warehouse rejected the 1906 Dunn shipment because its moisture measured 6.5 percent.

Keys to the reserve are held by the night clerk and the harbor master. The rule forbids leaving both keys with one person. Barrels that fail inspection are painted with a red band and sent back on the next daylight boat.

The warehouse does not store lamp oil. Lamp oil stays at the lighthouse cottage.
